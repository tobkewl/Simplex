What is Simplex?

Simplex is a powerful overlay tool for Path of Exile that enhances your trading and gameplay experience. It provides three main modules:

Live Searches + Whispers

Ready

Monitor live trade searches in real-time and manage incoming whispers in one place. Get instant alerts for new matches and keep trade conversations organized without leaving the overlay.

Build Guides

Ready

Follow curated build guides with level-by-level steps, skill trees, and gear milestones. Quickly preview what to do next and keep your progression on track.

Stash

Ready

Browse stash tabs and character inventory through server OAuth data. Item pricing and advanced net worth analytics are handled by website services.

Tip: You can access all settings and modules from the tray icon or by right-clicking the docking handle on the right side of your screen.
